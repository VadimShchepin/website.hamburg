Polish businesses in Germany

Polish businesses working in Germany almost always run into the same three questions: do I have to register for VAT here, does a permanent establishment arise, and where are my employees insured? The practice discusses this in Polish.

Common questions

Questions we hear often

Do I have to register for VAT in Germany?
Not in every case. For pure construction services to German businesses, the VAT liability shifts to the customer. For supplies to private customers, or your own warehouse in Germany, the position is different.
From when do I have a permanent establishment?
For a building site, under the treaty with Poland, after twelve months. Several related contracts at the same location can be added together.
Which language does the work happen in?
In Polish, if you prefer. Correspondence with the tax office naturally stays in German, and we translate it for you.
