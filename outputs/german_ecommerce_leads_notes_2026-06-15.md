## Outreach positioning

Do not lead with “we build online shops.” These prospects already have functioning shops.

Lead with one narrowly scoped commercial opportunity:

- A retailer or wholesale acquisition flow
- A product finder or guided-selling experience
- Recipe, routine, or educational content connected directly to the cart
- Replenishment, subscription, or post-purchase automation
- Seasonal gift and campaign landing pages
- Performance and mobile conversion improvements
- ERP, product-feed, fulfillment, analytics, or marketing integrations

Then position the Hamburg team as the partner that can handle the full implementation across strategy, design, development, content, automation, and ongoing optimization.

## Important Germany outreach note

Unsolicited promotional email is legally constrained in Germany. Section 7 UWG generally treats advertising by electronic mail without prior express consent as an unreasonable nuisance, subject to narrow exceptions. B2B status alone does not automatically make cold email permissible.

Official source: https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/uwg_2004/__7.html

Prefer relevant introductions, networking, LinkedIn engagement, trade events, expressly provided partnership/retailer routes, or a brief preliminary call where appropriate. Track consent, contact history, responses, and opt-outs.
