We work with DATEV

We prepare bookkeeping, payroll and annual accounts with the software of DATEV eG, the tax advisers' cooperative based in Nuremberg.

  • Payroll documents onlineYour employees download their payslips and certificates themselves in DATEV Arbeitnehmer online.
  • Data kept in GermanyYour data is stored in DATEV's data centre in Germany.
  • Common interfacesMany till, banking and inventory systems export data directly in DATEV format.
