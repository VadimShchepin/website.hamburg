03How it works

How an engagement begins

Three steps from first contact to ongoing work. What the engagement covers, and when the practice can start on it, is something we settle together in the first conversation.

01

Enquiry

You write briefly what the matter is. The questionnaire takes about five minutes and asks for legal form, scope and any deadlines that are running.

02

First conversation

In person at the practice in Wandsbek, or by phone. We go through what you would like to hand over, which parts the practice takes on and what to expect.

03

Working together

Power of attorney, notification to the tax office and collection of your records from the previous adviser. After that, bookkeeping, filings and deadlines sit with us.

Here you speak to the person who knows your file.
