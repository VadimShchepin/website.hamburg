The practice

A small practice on Schloßstraße in Wandsbek. Whoever calls speaks to someone who knows the file.

Photographs: Silke Heyer

The principal

Marek Hejzel

Marek Hejzel, born 1970, read business administration at the University of Hamburg. He then spent five years at a Big Four firm.

In 2004 he was admitted as a Steuerberater and set up on his own account the same year. He has run the practice on Schloßstraße in Hamburg Wandsbek ever since.

He decides on every engagement himself. That is the reason the practice has deliberately stayed small.

The team

Tax assistants, bookkeeping and trainees

The team consists of qualified tax assistants, a bookkeeper and trainees. They train continuously and are available to clients at any time.

They know the particulars of the many sectors and businesses they look after, often over many years. Their careful work is the foundation of the advice the practice gives.

The premises

Schloßstraße 108, first floor

Meetings take place at the practice, not in a conference centre. It is a short walk from Wandsbeker Markt; there is an ice cream cafe on the ground floor and the practice above it.
