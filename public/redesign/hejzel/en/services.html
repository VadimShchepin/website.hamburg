Services

The practice handles both the monthly work and the year-end work. What you hand over and what you keep is settled in the first conversation.

01

Tax advice

Returns, assessments, appeals and tax audits. We check what the tax office writes before you pay it.

  • Preparation of all tax returns: income tax, trade tax, corporation tax, VAT, inheritance and gift tax
  • Review of tax assessments
  • Negotiations with the tax office and formal appeals
  • Proceedings before the tax court
  • Support during tax audits
  • Opinions and reports on specific tax questions
02

Bookkeeping

Monthly or quarterly, with every filing. After each close you can see where the business stands, rather than finding out in the spring.

  • Monthly or quarterly bookkeeping
  • Advance VAT returns
  • EC sales lists
  • Cash basis profit calculation
  • Reports that show where the business actually earns
03

Annual accounts

From the commercial result to the tax balance sheet, through to electronic publication. We plan the dates backwards from the balance sheet date.

  • Annual accounts and the tax balance sheet derived from the commercial balance sheet
  • Profit calculation for sole traders and partnerships
  • Electronic publication of annual accounts
  • Valuation of work in progress and open items
  • Documents for the bank and for shareholders
04

Payroll

Calculation, registration, reporting. Including the certificates your staff have to produce for authorities and health insurers.

  • Wage and salary calculations
  • Payroll tax returns
  • Registrations, deregistrations and changes with the health insurers
  • Certificates for employees and authorities
  • Support during social security audits
05

Cross-border advice

For businesses with turnover, staff or clients abroad. Advice in Polish and English, and the correspondence stays with us.

  • Tax-efficient cross-border structuring
  • VAT registration and the reverse charge procedure
  • Construction services under section 13b of the German VAT Act for subcontractors
  • Permanent establishment, posting of workers and the evidence required
  • EC sales lists for goods moving within the EU
  • Meetings and correspondence in Polish or English
06

Starting a business

Legal form, registration questionnaire, small business scheme. The decisions of the first few weeks set the tax burden of the first few years.

  • Choice of legal form and its tax consequences
  • Registration with the tax office and the tax registration questionnaire
  • Small business scheme: the numbers before you commit
  • Setting up the bookkeeping from day one
  • Planning the first advance payments
