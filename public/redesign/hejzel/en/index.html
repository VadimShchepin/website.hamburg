How it works

How an engagement begins

Four steps from first contact to ongoing work. The third one is there on purpose.

01

Enquiry

You fill in the questionnaire. It takes about five minutes and settles up front what the matter is, how large the business is and which deadlines are running.

02

First conversation

In person at the practice in Wandsbek, or by phone. We tell you what we would take on, how it would run and what to expect.

03

Decision

Marek Hejzel decides himself whether the practice takes the engagement. If we are not the right address, we say so plainly, usually with a pointer to who is.

04

Handover

Power of attorney, notification to the tax office and collection of your records from the previous adviser. After that, bookkeeping, filings and deadlines sit with us.
