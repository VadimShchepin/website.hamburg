Common questions

Questions we hear often

I write the day's takings in a notebook. Is that enough?
If you do not use an electronic till, an open cash drawer with a daily cash report is permitted, but it has to be kept without gaps. As soon as an electronic till is in use, the technical requirements apply.
What happens in a cash inspection?
An inspector arrives unannounced and looks at the till and the records. If both hold up, the visit is over quickly.
How much of the tips do I have to tax?
Tips your staff receive directly from a customer are tax free for them. Tips that go to the business are business income.
