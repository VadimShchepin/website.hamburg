Private individuals and employees

Common questions

Questions we hear often

I have not filed a return for several years.
Where there was no obligation, you can usually catch up four years voluntarily. Where there was an obligation, it should be dealt with promptly. Both are solvable, and neither improves by waiting.
Is a return worth doing at all?
For employees it very often results in a refund. After a short look at your papers we will tell you whether the effort pays.
Do I have to file as a pensioner?
That depends on when the pension started, how large it is and what other income there is. Many pensioners are obliged to file without knowing it.
