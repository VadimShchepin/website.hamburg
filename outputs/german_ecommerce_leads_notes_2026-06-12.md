# German E-commerce Lead Research

Research date: 2026-06-12

The CSV contains 22 German small-to-medium e-commerce prospects. Each row includes a public business contact channel, a named owner/director where the shop publishes one, the legal/contact source, a personalization hook, and a likely service angle.

## Recommended workflow

1. Start with the `A` leads whose product positioning closely matches your strongest case studies.
2. Visit the website and source page again immediately before outreach, because roles and contact details can change.
3. Write a genuinely individual first message. Reference one specific feature from the personalization column and propose one narrowly scoped improvement.
4. Use general company inboxes only. Where only a support inbox is available, ask briefly who handles partnerships, marketing, or e-commerce instead of sending a full sales pitch.
5. Track the lawful basis, contact date, response, opt-out, and follow-up status in your CRM.

## Important Germany outreach note

Unsolicited promotional email is legally constrained in Germany. Section 7 of the German Act Against Unfair Competition (`UWG`) generally treats advertising by electronic mail without prior express consent as an unreasonable nuisance, subject to a narrow existing-customer exception. B2B status alone does not automatically make cold sales email permissible.

Official law source: https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/uwg_2004/__7.html

Before launching a campaign, get German legal advice and consider lower-risk approaches such as relevant introductions, networking, trade events, LinkedIn engagement, or a short call where a business-related presumed interest can be documented. Always honor opt-outs and do not contact a shop when its legal page expressly prohibits unsolicited advertising.

## Short personalization structure

Subject: Specific idea for `[shop / product category]`

Opening: Mention the exact product, program, collection, store, or positioning detail from the CSV.

Observation: Describe one concrete opportunity in one sentence, without pretending you have access to their analytics.

Proof: Add one relevant result or example from your work.

CTA: Ask whether the responsible person is open to a 15-minute conversation. Keep the first message short.
