Trades and construction

In construction it is not the value of the contract that decides whether there is money in the account at month end, it is the sequence of interim invoices, materials and wages. We run the bookkeeping so that you can see that sequence.

Invoices under § 13b UStG
If you supply a construction service to another construction business, that business owes the VAT, not you. A template is in the downloads section.
Exemption certificate under § 48b EStG
Without it your customer must withhold 15 per cent of the invoice and pay it to the tax office. We apply for the certificate and keep an eye on the renewal date.
Interim and final invoices
Every interim payment belongs in the advance VAT return for the right month, and the final invoice has to set them off correctly.
Payroll for site staff
Calculation, payroll tax filings and registrations with the health insurance funds, including the particulars of the construction sector where they apply.
Work in progress at the year end
Contracts running across the balance sheet date have to be valued. It is one of the first things a tax audit looks at.
Tools, vehicles and workwear
What is deductible immediately and what is spread over the useful life. For vehicles there is also the choice between a logbook and the one per cent rule.

Common questions

Questions we hear often

When do I issue an invoice without VAT?
When your customer also supplies construction services, the VAT liability shifts to them. You show no VAT and instead refer to § 13b UStG on the invoice. A ready-made template is in the downloads.
My customer withheld 15 per cent. Is that money gone?
No, it is credited against your taxes. It is still missing from your bank account in the meantime. With a valid exemption certificate it does not happen at all.
My receipts are photographs on my phone.
That is a normal starting point. What matters is that they are fully legible and arrive regularly, rather than once a year in a box.
